Only just a couple notes this go around-

1) some of the secrets have region names attached to give hints. Regrettably, not all of them, but some secrets are more obtuse than others. A couple got marked as unattainable either because they didn't trigger or ten minutes of searching didn't reveal the button. Generally, secrets that were pretty self-evident didn't get region hints. Like, shoot Hitler's picture in the bonus levels. You were gonna do that anyways, because fuck that guy.
2) Map29 got a little bit weird about spawning the enemies necessary to produce the orange skull key. I'm blaming Colourful Hell for this one, but it's worth a mention.
3) Every map needs a red key to finish it. Usually you need the other keys to get TO the red key, but since the exit is always at the start, you COULD complete a level with just the red key and not the other requisite keys, assuming you broke logic somehow. Also, 90% of the time the red key is used exclusively to complete the level, so there's not a whole lot to 'get' from that, I suppose.
4) Options.py has been adjusted to use UV. Allow Respawn has been defaulted to False, since there are several levels where respawning soft-locks the player out of parts or the whole stage.
5) It's really fun! Maps are short and different enough to stay refreshing and the checks come pretty quick. Avoids being a switch hunt, for the most part.
6) Players are advised to avoid triggering the exit elevator until they're 100% done with the level, since the elevator lifts past the entrance to the level and traps the player unless noclipping is used to escape.

Link to megawad: https://www.doomworld.com/idgames/levels/doom2/Ports/megawads/helevtor
Bug notes from that page: "	We tried really hard to track down and fix monsters not teleporting in, but still could have missed some. Due to maps' structure multiplayer might have softlocks, although we fixed most egregious cases. MAP22 is going to break down in multiplayer if different players walk into multiple rooms at once. It can't be helped."
