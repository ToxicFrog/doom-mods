All powerups, large healing items, weapons, secrets and keys checks
have been given custom names. If you play with hints, hinting for any of
the aforementioned checks should give you a better idea where to look
for said check, as well as info if they require keys or secrets.

There are a couple places that are either secrets but not marked as such, you
may only have one attempt to reach or are otherwise difficult to reach:

MAP02 - one attempt jump
MAP04 - unmarked secret dark door,
MAP19 - unmarked secret close to rocket launcher,
MAP24 - archvile jump BFG
MAP25 - Multi-zap jump area in BFG arena.

These locations have been marked inaccessible.
